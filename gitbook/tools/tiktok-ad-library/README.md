# TikTok Ad Library

## URL

[https://library.tiktok.com/ads](https://library.tiktok.com/ads)&#x20;

Last checked: 2026-08-02

## Description

[TikTok Ad Library](https://library.tiktok.com/ads) is the paid-ad section of TikTok’s [Commercial Content Library](https://library.tiktok.com/). It lets anyone search ads shown to users in the EEA, Switzerland, and the UK and review the advertiser, payer, first and last shown dates, estimated reach range, disclosed targeting criteria, and country-level reach.&#x20;

No TikTok account is required. [TikTok Support](https://www.tiktok.com/support/faq_detail?id=7636670088171002389)&#x20;

Investigators can use the Ad Library to compare messaging, reach, and advertising activity across advertisers, examine disclosed targeting criteria, and preserve creative and metadata before the library’s retention periods expire. Paid ads may stop appearing in feeds when campaigns end, while creator promotions may be deleted or made private. TikTok keeps qualifying records in the library for a limited period, described below.

TikTok launched the [Commercial Content Library in 2023](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/expanding-tiktoks-research-api-and-commercial-content-library?lang=en-150) as an advertising-transparency tool. Treat it as a platform-provided investigative source rather than a complete or independently verified record.

[A simple workflow](https://www.tiktok.com/support/faq_detail?id=7636670088171002389): choose **Ad Library**, select **Ad target country**, optionally set **Ad type** and **Publication date**, then search by advertiser name or keyword. Open an ad’s details to review the advertiser, “ad paid for by”, first and last shown dates, estimated unique-user range, targeting summary, and country-level reach. Ads can take up to 24 hours to appear after their first view or a campaign change. If TikTok removed an ad for a policy violation, some metadata may remain while the creative is hidden.

The separate [All ads report](https://library.tiktok.com/ads/report) aggregates ad activity by country, date, and advertiser and visualizes ads published by country and day. TikTok warns that changes can take up to 48 hours to appear in these reports.

## Requirements

* No login required for the web interface; it works in a modern desktop browser.&#x20;
* Optional: A [separate Commercial Content API](https://developers.tiktok.com/products/commercial-content-api) is available to approved researchers; it is not required for manual use.

## Limitations

* [Geographic scope](https://www.tiktok.com/support/faq_detail?id=7636670088171002389): includes ads targeted to users in the EEA, Switzerland, and the UK; other regions are not included.&#x20;
* [Time scope](https://www.tiktok.com/support/faq_detail?id=7636670088171002389): includes ads published on or after 2022-10-01. Paid ads are retained for one year after their last view. Qualifying creator promotions remain for one year after the creator deletes them or makes them private.
* Metrics are coarse; audience numbers are provided as ranges and targeting is summarised.&#x20;
* Delay: individual ads and campaign changes can take up to 24 hours to appear. The aggregate **All ads report** warns that changes can take up to 48 hours to be reflected.&#x20;
* Regulatory and completeness caveat: the European Commission preliminarily identified gaps in the repository’s searchability and ad, payer, targeting and reach information. On 2025-12-05, it made TikTok’s 23 remedial commitments binding for 5 years and closed the case. The commitments include additional filters, a higher cap on fuzzy-search results, richer ad and targeting fields, and faster updates. [Check the official decision](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A52026DSA100109) and confirm which fields and limits are available during collection; do not treat the results as exhaustive.&#x20;
* **Other commercial content** means creator-posted content that promotes a brand, product, or service but that TikTok was not paid to distribute. It appears in the library only when the creator uses TikTok’s commercial-content disclosure setting, so undisclosed sponsorships may be missing.
* The site is a JavaScript app; some pages can be more challenging to forensically archive than static pages. Archiving dynamic pages can be inconsistent; independent snapshots may miss embedded media; test your archiving method per investigation.

## Ethical Considerations

* Respect platform terms and local law; avoid republishing ad media with sensitive targeting without context.
* Preserve provenance; when archiving, capture URLs and timestamps; consider adding independent web archive snapshots.
